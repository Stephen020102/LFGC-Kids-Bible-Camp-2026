# Church Event Website Kit

A drop-in content pack for your conference/retreat website. Built for **Wix (multi-page)** with a **bright & energetic** style. Every section below is ready to paste into Wix — just replace the placeholders in `[BRACKETS]` with your real details.

**Placeholders to find-and-replace before you publish:**

- `[EVENT NAME]` — the title of your conference/retreat
- `[THEME]` — your theme or tagline (e.g., "Rooted & Rising")
- `[DATE]` — full date or date range (e.g., "Oct 17–19, 2026")
- `[TIME]` — start/end times
- `[LOCATION]` — venue name and address
- `[CHURCH NAME]` — your church
- ~~`[GOOGLE FORM URL]`~~ ✅ **Set to:** https://forms.gle/vEqfyuUpi2EJBM78A
- `[CONTACT EMAIL]` — the email people can reach you at

- `[PRICE]` — registration cost, or "Free"
- `[REGISTRATION DEADLINE]` — last day to sign up

## 1. Site Map (Pages in your Wix menu)

A six-page structure that's clean, easy to navigate, and shows up well on mobile. In Wix's left sidebar, create these pages in this order:

1. **Home** — the landing page; the first thing visitors see
2. **About** — what the event is, who it's for, why it matters
3. **Schedule** — what happens each day/session
4. **Speakers** — featured speakers, worship leaders, or hosts
5. **Register** — the Google Form embedded right on the page
6. **Contact / FAQ** — questions, directions, who to call

If you want to keep it leaner, you can merge "Speakers" into "About" and "Contact" into "FAQ" — that gets you to four pages.

## 3. Color Palette & Style

### Primary palette — "Sunrise" (recommended)

This is the bright, energetic palette I'd reach for. Warm and inviting, but with enough punch to feel alive.

| Role | Color | Hex code | Where to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Coral | `#FF6B5B` | Main buttons, headline accents, the big CTA band |
| **Secondary** | Marigold yellow | `#FFB627` | Highlights, icons, secondary buttons |
| **Accent** | Sky blue | `#2EC4F1` | Links, hover states, decorative shapes |
| **Dark text / base** | Charcoal | `#1A1A2E` | Body text, footer background |
| **Light background** | Off-white | `#FFF8F2` | Page background |
| **Pure white** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, contrast on dark sections |

### Alternative palette 1 — "Festival" (more playful)

Hot pink `#FF3D7F` · Lime `#B6FF3C` · Royal blue `#2C3FFF` · Cream `#FFF6E5` · Black `#0F0F12`

### Alternative palette 2 — "Sunset Worship" (warmer, more reverent)

Tangerine `#FF7A1A` · Rose `#FF4F8B` · Teal `#00C2C7` · Indigo `#1B1F4B` · White `#FFFFFF`

### Typography

Use two free Google Fonts (both available natively in Wix):

- **Headlines / display:** Outfit, Bricolage Grotesque, or Space Grotesk — bold weight (700–800)
- **Body text:** Inter or Manrope — regular (400) for body, semi-bold (600) for emphasis

Rule of thumb: headlines big and confident, body text comfortable and a touch larger than you think (16–18px on desktop, 16px on mobile).

### Visual style rules

A few principles to keep the design from looking busy:

- **Lots of white (or off-white) space.** Don't fill every pixel.
- **One photo per section, max.** Hero image on Home, one image per content row elsewhere.
- **Use color in chunks.** Big colored bands (the CTA strip, the footer) — not lots of small colored elements competing.
- **Rounded corners, not sharp.** Set buttons and cards to a 12–16px border radius.
- **Bold short headlines, friendly long-form body.** No corporate-speak.

### Photography guidance

For a bright, energetic vibe, look for photos that show:

- People mid-laugh, mid-worship, mid-conversation (not posed)
- Outdoor / golden-hour light
- Hands raised, instruments, candid crowd shots
- Empty-room shots of your venue with sunlight streaming in

Free sources: **Unsplash** (search "worship," "youth conference," "community") and **Lightstock** (paid but built for churches).

## 4. How to Build This in Wix (step by step)

Since you don't code, here's the no-code path. You'll be done in a few hours.

### Step 1 — Pick a template

In Wix, go to **Choose a Template → Events → Conferences & Meetups**. Pick any template close to the structure above. Don't worry about matching exactly — you'll customize it.

### Step 2 — Set your colors and fonts globally

In the Wix editor, click **Site Design** (left sidebar) → **Color** → click "Edit." Paste in your hex codes from the palette above into the five preset slots. Then click **Text Themes** and set your headline and body fonts to the ones listed above. Doing this *first* means every section automatically picks up your style.

### Step 3 — Build the pages

In the left sidebar, click **Pages & Menu**. You'll already have a Home page. Add five more pages by clicking the + button:

- About
- Schedule
- Speakers
- Register
- Contact

For each page, paste in the matching content from Section 2 above. Wix has drag-and-drop sections — pick "Hero," "Text & Image," "Grid," "Accordion" (for FAQ), etc.

### Step 4 — Connect your registration form

Your form URL: **https://forms.gle/vEqfyuUpi2EJBM78A**

Use this in two places: (1) the embedded form on the Register page, and (2) every "Register" / "Save My Spot" button across the site.

**A. Embed the form on the Register page (so people stay on your site):**

1. Open your Google Form: https://forms.gle/vEqfyuUpi2EJBM78A
2. Click **Send** (top right) → click the `<>` icon (Embed HTML).
3. Copy the iframe code Google gives you.
4. In Wix, go to the Register page → click **+ Add → Embed Code → Embed HTML**.
5. Paste the code in. Resize the iframe to fill the page width (set height to about 1200px).

**B. Point every "Register" button to the form URL:**

For each button on Home, About, and the CTA bands:

1. In Wix, click the button → click **Link**.
2. Choose **Web Address**.
3. Paste: `https://forms.gle/vEqfyuUpi2EJBM78A`
4. Toggle **Opens in:** New tab (recommended).
5. Click **Done**.

Repeat for every Register Now / Save My Spot button (there are three in the layout).

### Step 5 — Mobile check (don't skip this)

In the Wix editor, click the phone icon at the top of the screen. Walk through every page. Adjust any text that's getting cut off, any images that look weird, and any buttons that are too small to tap.

### Step 6 — Connect your domain (optional but recommended)

If you have a domain like `yourchurch.org`, you can point a subdomain (`event.yourchurch.org`) to this Wix site. In Wix: **Settings → Domains → Connect a Domain You Already Own** and follow the instructions.

### Step 7 — Publish

Hit **Publish** (top right). Done.
